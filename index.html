Steph Curry

The first key player on the Golden State Warriors is Stephen Curry. He has been playing on the warriors for more than ten years, he has the most three pointers with 4,286 thousand threes in NBA history, he has won multiple MVP awards and 4 NBA championships, and the olympic gold medal in 2024 with team U.S.A. This season he is averaging 30.6 points a game, 4.1 rebounds, and 4.3 assists a game at 38 years old. He is averaging the second most points in the NBA right now is he below Luka doncic and is higher then last years MVP Shai Gilgeous Alexander who is averaging around 29 points per game. Steph curry is one of the main reasons the warriors are going on this 8 game winning streak.

Updates on Steph Curry
