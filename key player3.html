Draymond Green

Draymond Green is an American professional basketball player for the Golden State Warriors, widely recognized as one of the greatest defensive players and playmaking forwards of his era.Now in his 15th NBA season 2026 27, Green remains the defensive anchor and emotional leader for Golden State alongside longtime teammate Stephen Curry.
